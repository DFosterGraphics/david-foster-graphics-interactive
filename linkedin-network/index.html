February 2007
One node. Eighteen years of colleagues.
I joined LinkedIn in February 2007, still at PC Magazine — a job I'd held for 18 years. Within months, 195 colleagues appeared, formalizing relationships that stretched back to 1989.

The blue cluster forming in the upper left is everything I'd built. I didn't know yet that I was about to lose it.
Job · PC Magazine
October 2007
The stream stops.
PC Magazine shut down its print edition. After 18 years, I was out. Watch the blue node stream slow, then cut off almost entirely.

The recession was coming. Media was imploding. The network held its breath.
Job · PC Magazine ends
2008 – 2011
BusinessWeek. Fortune. Refusing to leave journalism.
Two more mastheads. Brief stints, but significant ones. BusinessWeek (2008–2010), then Fortune (2010–2011). New blue clusters forming, smaller than PC Magazine but dense.

The purple music world kept growing through all of it — a parallel life that never stopped.
Job · BusinessWeek + Fortune
2011
The pivot. Everything explodes.
I joined Vindico, an ad tech startup. A complete industry change — from editorial to programmatic advertising.

438 connections in a single year. Watch the network detonate. New colors, new world, new velocity. The pivot that saved everything.
Job · Vindico / Viant
2017
The return.
Six years in ad tech, then Yahoo Finance called. Back to journalism. Back to data visualization. This time with a deeper toolkit and a clearer identity.

Eight years. The longest chapter since PC Magazine.
Job · Yahoo Finance
September 2024

The Online News Association conference. Watch the green Conference / Industry cluster light up almost overnight — dataviz journalists, editors, developers from across the field.

The community I'd been part of for years, finally formalized.

Fall 2025
The layoff. The LinkedIn surge.
Yahoo Finance. Eight years. Gone.

Watch what happens to the gray LinkedIn Connection cluster. The network doesn't mourn — it mobilizes. Recruiters. Colleagues. Strangers who know the work. All arriving at once.

About this visualization

Each dot represents a LinkedIn connection, colored by how I know them and animated in the order we connected — from February 2007 to today. Names are anonymized to protect privacy.

Built with D3.js and Scrollama. Data tagged manually over several weeks, one connection at a time.
